Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly can you connect me with talent?

We typically present qualified candidates within 1-2 weeks of understanding your requirements. The exact timeline depends on the specific skills and experience level needed.

What is your vetting process for talent?

Our rigorous vetting includes technical assessments, portfolio reviews, cultural fit evaluation, and background verification to ensure only top-tier talent joins your team.

Do you offer project-based or full-time placements?

We offer both project-based engagements and full-time placements, tailored to your business needs and project requirements.

How do you handle cultural and communication differences?

Our German project managers act as cultural bridges, ensuring smooth communication and alignment between German businesses and Nigerian talent through regular check-ins and clear processes.
