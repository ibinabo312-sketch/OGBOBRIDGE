Our Story

OgboBridge was founded with a vision to create meaningful connections between the thriving tech ecosystems of Germany and Nigeria.

The Vision

Our founders recognized the immense potential of Nigerian tech talent and the growing demand for skilled developers in the German market. We saw an opportunity to bridge these two worlds with a focus on quality, understanding, and mutual benefit.

Where We Are Now

As a new startup, we're currently building our foundation. We've established our rigorous vetting process and are actively developing relationships with both German companies seeking talent and Nigerian developers looking for international opportunities.
